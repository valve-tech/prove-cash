# App review — 2026-09-30

A full review of `app/src` at master `85cbaf1`. It is not a diff review. An agent did it
by reading the code. It ran nothing and changed nothing. Each finding still needs
confirmation before a fix.

**Findings: 0 critical, 3 high, 5 medium, 1 low.**

## High

**H1. The Mine tab keeps the old account's rows after an account switch. The bulk off
switch then acts on the new account.**
`components/Portfolio.tsx:126-137, 139-256, 316-329, 491-502`; `App.tsx:1056`.
`Portfolio` is keyed by `chainId` only. `load()` has no stale guard and no `catch`.
`rows`, `parked`, `floor` and `confirming` are not reset when `maker` changes.
A user switches from account A to account B. A's rows stay on screen with live buttons.
"Turn every offer off" lists A's offers, but the click sends `invalidateBefore(B, floorFromA)`.
That retires B's lane-0 offers, which the list never showed. The action is irreversible
(APP-MINE-5). Fix: key by `chainId:account`, or add a per-load token, and catch errors.

**H2. Money figures use the SDK's en-US `fmt` in every locale.**
`BatchTray.tsx:92`; `Portfolio.tsx:424,509,730,768,772`; `rfq/QuoteCard.tsx:221-240,301,310`;
`rfq/SettleQuote.tsx:222-234`; `TokenPicker.tsx:115`; `fields.tsx:165`; source
`sdk/src/tokens.ts:318-327`. `lib/i18n.ts:420-433` bans this pattern. A German reader
reads `1,234.5` as 1.2345 on the fill, quote and escrow screens. Fix: use `units()` from
`lib/amount.ts`.

**H3. The RFQ surfaces do not hold the chain while they sign.**
`rfq/Rfq.tsx:128-150`; `rfq/MakeQuote.tsx:232-305`; `rfq/QuoteCard.tsx:142-181`;
`rfq/SettleQuote.tsx:92-103`; `App.tsx:224, 316-325, 603, 1053`.
Only `Compose` calls `onBusy`, so the network menu stays enabled during an RFQ flow.
`signAndArm` and `signIntent` read `getChainId()` at each step. A switch from 943 to
369 mid-flow signs a 943 intent under 369's domain and sends the approval and deposit to
369's relayer. HEX has one address on both chains (SDK-TOK-1). Fix: pass `onBusy`
through `Rfq`, and take the chain once per submit.

## Medium

**M1. The account listener binds once, to the wallet present at mount.**
`App.tsx:296`; `lib/wallet.ts:145-148, 223-226, 479-500`. `preferWallet` and
`adoptSession` do not move it. After a wallet change, the header shows a stale account
or another wallet's account, and forms sign with that `maker`.

**M2. The raw board error goes into `title={error}`** at `App.tsx:872` (APP-ERR-2).
`useBoard.ts:603` keeps `e.message` unredacted, and `firstLine` only cuts at a newline.
A viem error carries the RPC URL, so a keyed custom endpoint shows its key in the DOM.

**M3. The approval rung can sign without an allowance.**
`lib/arm.ts:112`; `hooks/useCustody.ts:54-60, 75`; `Compose.tsx:417-426`.
`needsApproval` stays `null` while the read is pending or after it throws.
`signAndArm` skips the approval on `null`, and `ready` does not wait for the read.
On a slow RPC, a maker posts an offer that every fill then fails.

**M4. The fill tray cannot arm a Permit2 offer, and Mine labels it "wallet".**
`BatchTray.tsx:161-176, 186-190, 203-204`; `IntentRow.tsx:97-109`; `Portfolio.tsx:197`.
The UI can sign Permit2 offers but cannot fill them: the fill runs `direct`, the
simulation fails, and the row shows "fail". The reviewer found this by reading the code
and did not run it.

**M5. Three user-facing claims are false** (APP-CLAIM, the 2026-08-12 claims spec).
- `i18n/en.ts:893` (`seo.description`) says "costs no gas and no transaction". The
  escrow rung costs one or two transactions.
- The same string says "you receive exactly the rate you signed". The fee comes out of
  the signed gross.
- `en.ts:717` (`tour.cancel.body`) says a wallet offer "expires within the hour". The
  form signs windows of up to 24 hours.
- `en.ts:934` (`landing.hero.lede`) says "no transaction from you". This is false on
  the approval, escrow and Permit2 rungs.

All of these strings exist in all 35 locales.

## Low

**L1.** `Compose.tsx:699` shows the floor rate with `Number(rate.toPrecision(6))`. Values
under 1e-6 print as `1.23e-7`, and the locale decimal mark is ignored. It is display only.

## Checked and clean

- Secrets in the bundle.
- XSS and unsafe HTML.
- Landing isolation (APP-LAND-1).
- Sign-form amounts, decimals and fee maths.
- Rung locks (SDK-INT-3, SDK-CUS-3).
- `ensureChain`.
- Null before the first answer (APP-BRD-4).
- Clearing state when the board chain changes.
- Remounting `Compose` and `Rfq`.
- The fill tray gates (APP-FILL-1, APP-FILL-2).
- Withdraw and cancel on the Mine tab.

## Residual risk

- `TokenPicker.tsx:243-248` registers decimals from a third-party token list without an
  on-chain read. This conflicts with the spirit of SDK-TOK-2.
- `useRfqKey.ts:58-67` has a narrow race: a key derived for account A can be written
  after a switch to account B.
- APP-SIGN-1 h and APP-SIGN-2 call the fee-sink redesign "not built". It is built. The
  spec text is stale, not the code.

## Not reviewed

WalletPicker, Tour, the charts, the landing console, the speech modules, and the i18n
parity tests.
