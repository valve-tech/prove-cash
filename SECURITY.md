# Reporting a bug or a security hole

Send it to us over SimpleX chat:

https://smp12.simplex.im/a#bU9ltr40p0c5WoAxDzKy1RdSqhcwi3EqQkduz-_EusU

SimpleX needs no account, email or phone number. Open the link in the SimpleX app,
or open it in a browser and follow the steps it shows.

Please do not post a security hole anywhere public before we have answered you.

## What to include

- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
- The chain (369 or 943), and any contract address or transaction hash involved.
- For the app: the page address, your browser, and a screenshot if you have one.

## What is in scope

- The contracts listed in `contracts/deployments/369.json` and `943.json`.
- The app at https://prove.cash.
- The SDK in `sdk/`.

Test against testnet (943) where you can. Never move funds that are not yours.

The same address is published at https://prove.cash/.well-known/security.txt.
